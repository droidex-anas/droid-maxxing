## What’s new

- Keep long conversations responsive with a virtualized timeline, incremental streaming Markdown, and cheaper live feed updates.
- Open DROIDEX faster through renderer code splitting, deferred main-process work, and a sidecar that reports ready as soon as the bridge is listening.
- Toggle hardware acceleration in Settings and restart when you want the change to take effect.
- Review pull requests across every open workspace, with worktrees of the same repository collapsed and a dead remote isolated to that repo.
- Remove a workspace from the sidebar with a right-click or two-finger tap; chats from that folder stay in history.
- Find and copy ranges in a conversation from transcript state instead of the rendered DOM.
- See subagent cards as soon as a worker or validator starts, then follow their stream without rebuilding the whole chat.
- Recover from sidecar crashes without quitting DROIDEX, and keep Send available even when no sidecar supervisor exists yet.

## Updating from 1.1.5

DROIDEX 1.1.6 keeps the existing application identity, user-data location, session store, settings, workspaces, and raw Factory session history unchanged. The native updater waits for confirmation before restarting when a primary or child session is active.

Use **DROIDEX → Check for Updates…** or the blue update button beside Settings. The macOS artifacts are ad-hoc signed, so a manual DMG install may require approval in **System Settings → Privacy & Security** on first launch.
