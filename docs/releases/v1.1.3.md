## What’s new

- Turn explanations, datasets, diagrams, and lessons into responsive interactive App blocks directly inside chat with `/visualize`.
- Continue refining an App naturally in the same conversation without repeating the command, then replay restored Apps safely from their compact preview.
- Use the cleaner compact composer menu, clearer session attention badges, and session-scoped permission and question prompts.
- Keep long or streamed App responses stable across live rendering, chat restore, theme changes, and narrow window sizes.

## Updating from 1.1.2

DROIDEX 1.1.3 keeps the existing session store and user-data location unchanged. Existing chats, child sessions, settings, workspaces, and raw Factory session history remain in place. Quit DROIDEX before installing, then use **DROIDEX → Check for Updates…** or download the DMG that matches your Mac.

The macOS artifacts are ad-hoc signed. A manual DMG install may require approval in **System Settings → Privacy & Security** on first launch.
