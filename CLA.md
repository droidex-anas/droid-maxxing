# DROIDEX Contributor Copyright Assignment Agreement

This agreement applies to each contribution you intentionally submit to the
official DROIDEX repository. "You" means the individual or legal entity making
the contribution. "Project Owner" means the copyright holder identified in
[LICENSE](LICENSE). "Contribution" means the copyrightable material you submit
for inclusion in DROIDEX. "Accepted Contribution" means a Contribution the
Project Owner merges into an official DROIDEX branch.

In exchange for the Project Owner reviewing your Contribution and, if accepted,
including it in DROIDEX, you agree:

1. **Assignment.** When your Contribution becomes an Accepted Contribution,
   you irrevocably assign to the Project Owner all worldwide right, title, and
   interest in it for the full duration of those rights, including all
   copyrights and the exclusive rights to use, reproduce, modify, publish,
   distribute, sublicense, sell, commercialize, enforce, and create derivative
   works from it.
2. **Consideration and royalties.** The Project Owner's review and potential
   inclusion of your Contribution, and the opportunity to participate in the
   project, are the agreed consideration for this assignment. No payment or
   royalty is owed for an Accepted Contribution, to the extent permitted by
   applicable law.
3. **Fallback license.** If any part of the assignment is ineffective under
   applicable law, you grant the Project Owner an exclusive, perpetual,
   worldwide, irrevocable, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free license to
   exercise those rights for any purpose.
4. **Further assurance.** You will reasonably assist with documents needed to
   confirm or record the assignment. The Project Owner will reimburse any
   reasonable pre-approved out-of-pocket cost of doing so.
5. **Moral rights.** To the extent permitted by law, you waive and agree not to
   assert moral rights or similar personal rights in an Accepted Contribution.
6. **Your authority.** You confirm that the Contribution is your original work
   or that you have sufficient rights to submit it and make this assignment.
   You also confirm that it does not knowingly violate another person's
   copyright, patent, trade secret, contract, or other rights.
7. **Third-party material.** You will clearly identify any third-party material
   and its license. You will not submit third-party material unless the Project
   Owner has approved it in writing.
8. **No obligation.** The Project Owner is not required to use, merge, publish,
   or compensate you for a Contribution. A rejected Contribution remains yours,
   but the DROIDEX license continues to restrict your use of DROIDEX material
   contained in it.
9. **Electronic acceptance.** Entering your full legal name and checking the
   agreement box in a DROIDEX pull request is your electronic signature and
   acceptance of this agreement. The pull request and its GitHub audit history
   identify the Contribution covered by that acceptance.

Do not submit a Contribution if you do not accept these terms or lack authority
to accept them, including when an employer or client owns your work.
